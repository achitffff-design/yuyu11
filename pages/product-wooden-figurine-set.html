Frequently Asked Questions

What wood are the figurines made from?

They're carved from solid FSC-certified rubberwood — a sustainable hardwood harvested at the end of the rubber tree's productive life. No MDF, no veneer, no toxic finishes.

Are they suitable for children?

The figurines are decorative objects, not toys. The finish is food-safe and non-toxic, but small parts and pointed forms make them unsuitable for children under 3. Keep out of reach.

Will the wood color vary?

Yes — natural wood has inherent grain and tonal variation, which we celebrate. Each set is unique; minor differences are a hallmark of authentic handcraft.

Can I order custom figurine designs?

Yes. We offer custom carving from your sketches or references (MOQ: 100 sets). We can also adapt poses, sizes and finishes for wholesale. Contact wholesale@artisancraft-global.com.

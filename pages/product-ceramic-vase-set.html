Frequently Asked Questions

Is the ceramic vase set safe for food use?

Yes, all our ceramic products are lead-free, cadmium-free and food-safe certified. They can hold beverages, dry goods or serve as decorative pieces. We don't recommend storing acidic foods for extended periods as this may affect the glaze over time.

About ArtisanCraft Global

We connect skilled artisans with homes, boutiques and brands around the world — celebrating handmade craft, one piece at a time.

Our Story

ArtisanCraft Global began with a simple belief: that the objects we live with every day should carry the warmth and character of a human hand. In 2018, our founder — a product designer who had spent years sourcing mass-produced goods for big-box retailers — visited a small pottery studio in Jingdezhen, China, the historic "porcelain capital." Watching a single vase take shape over hours on a wheel, the contrast with factory-perfect uniformity was immediate and humbling.

That trip became the seed of our company. We started with one ceramic collection and a handful of wholesale partners. Today, we work with artisan workshops across Jingdezhen, Bali, rural Vietnam and northern India — sourcing ceramics, woven fiber, candles, woodcraft and embroidered textiles. We are still small enough to know our makers by name, and growing enough to bring their work to customers in over 80 countries.

What We Believe

Craft Over Quantity

We'd rather sell fewer pieces made with care than thousands of identical ones. Every item is hand-finished, and we celebrate the small variations that make each piece unique.

Fair to Makers

We partner directly with workshops — no exploitative middlemen. Makers receive fair prices, stable orders and lead times that respect seasonal and agricultural rhythms.

Kind to the Planet

Natural and biodegradable materials, plastic-free packaging, and sea-freight options for bulk orders to cut emissions. We are not perfect, but we are always improving.

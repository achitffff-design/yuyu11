Frequently Asked Questions

Does it come with a cushion insert?

No, this listing is for the cover only, which keeps shipping compact and eco-friendly. It fits any standard 45×45cm insert — down, feather or synthetic — widely available from home stores.

How should I care for the embroidery?

Machine wash on a 30°C gentle cycle, ideally inside a laundry bag to protect the embroidery. Line dry and warm iron. Avoid bleach. The cotton embroidery thread is colorfast when washed cool.

Will the embroidery be identical on every cover?

No — because each cover is hand-embroidered, slight variations in stitch density and motif detail are normal and expected. These differences are the signature of genuine handcraft.

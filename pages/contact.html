Quick Answers

How long does shipping take?

Standard international shipping takes 10–20 business days; express shipping (DHL/FedEx) takes 3–7 business days. See individual product pages for details or visit our Products page.

Do you offer wholesale and custom orders?

Yes. We support custom colors, private-label engraving and bespoke packaging with MOQ as low as 50 units. Lead time is typically 4–6 weeks. Email wholesale@artisancraft-global.com for a quote.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy on all retail orders. Custom and wholesale orders are final sale but are covered by a 90-day quality warranty.

Are your ceramics food-safe?

All our ceramics are lead-free, cadmium-free and certified to FDA and LFGB standards. Learn more on our About Us page.

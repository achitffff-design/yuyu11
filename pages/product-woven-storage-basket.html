Frequently Asked Questions

Are the baskets sturdy enough for heavy items?

Yes. The tight weave and integrated handles make them suitable for throws, books, toys and pantry goods. We recommend keeping individual loads under 5kg to preserve the woven structure over time.

Is the cotton lining removable and washable?

Yes, the lining is fully removable and machine washable at 30°C. Tumble dry low or line dry to prevent shrinkage.

Will the rattan color vary between baskets?

Slight tonal variation is normal and expected with natural rattan — it's part of the organic character. Each basket is unique, just like the artisan who wove it.

Can I order custom sizes or colors for wholesale?

Yes. We offer custom dimensions, dyed rattan colors and printed cotton linings for wholesale orders (MOQ: 50 sets). Contact wholesale@artisancraft-global.com for details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these candles safe for pets?

Our soy wax and lead-free cotton wicks are a cleaner choice than paraffin. However, some essential oils can be irritating to pets — we recommend burning in a well-ventilated room and keeping pets away from direct exposure. If unsure, consult your veterinarian.

How do I get the best burn?

On the first burn, let the wax pool reach the edges (about 2 hours) to prevent tunneling. Trim the wick to 5mm before each light, and never burn for more than 4 hours at a time.

Can I reuse the ceramic vessels?

Absolutely. Once the candle is finished, wash out remaining wax with warm soapy water (or freeze for 10 minutes to pop it out). The vessels are food-safe, dishwasher safe and perfect for small plants, stationary or snacks.

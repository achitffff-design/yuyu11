Frequently Asked Questions

Will the brass inlay tarnish?

Brass develops a natural patina over time, which many find beautiful. To keep it bright, wipe occasionally with a brass-polishing cloth. The patina does not affect durability.

How much jewelry can it hold?

The removable tray comfortably holds 8–10 rings and 10–15 pairs of earrings, with the lower compartment for larger items like necklaces, bracelets and watches.

Is the velvet lining removable for cleaning?

The tray lifts out, but the velvet is fixed to the tray. Spot-clean gently with a damp cloth and mild soap; do not soak.

Can I customize the inlay or add a logo?

Yes. We offer custom brass inlay patterns and logo engraving for wholesale (MOQ: 100 boxes). Contact wholesale@artisancraft-global.com for design options.
